Zero effort

Three taps from a folder of photos
to a film worth sharing.

You already did the hard part — you took the photos. Roamango does everything else, using the location and dates already baked into your library.

1

It scans

Roamango reads the GPS coordinates and timestamps already stored in your photos — read-only, right on the device.

2

It reconstructs

Photos cluster into stops and stops into trips. Your home base is detected and left out, so only real journeys remain.

3

You roam

Tap once and Roamango animates the route across the map, blooming your best shots at every stop. We call each one a roam.

a living map of your life

Every journey, on a spinning globe.

Open the app and your travels are already there — pinned to a 3D globe with a real photo from each trip. Spin it, filter by year, and tap any pin to dive in.

  • Trips, Build Your Own & Stats — jump straight to a detected trip or craft a custom date range.
  • Year filter — sweep through all your memories to today and watch the pins change.

automatic, but yours to shape

Full control, only if you want it.

Roamango's defaults just work. But every trip opens into a full-bleed map editor where you can fine-tune the story down to the stop.

  • Add, reorder, merge or delete stops
  • Merge whole trips, or split them apart
  • Quick Roam for a fast preview, HD Roam for the final cut

how your photos land

Choose the look of every reveal.

Photos can drift over the map as polaroid postcards, or take over the whole screen edge-to-edge. Then pick how they arrive.

  • Postcard or Full Screen, single or double per stop
  • Reveal animations — Balloon, Drop, Fan, Slide Stack
  • Transitions — Push, Dissolve, Wipe, Zoom

the right shots, not the random ones

Your best photos, chosen for you.

A tiered scoring engine ranks every photo at each stop, so your highlights bloom and the throwaways stay hidden. Don't agree? Pick your own in a tap.

  • Favourites, edits and album picks rank highest
  • Portraits, panoramas and Apple's own curation count too
  • Screenshots and duplicates filtered out automatically

set the mood

Add your voice. Add a soundtrack.

Record narration straight over the roam as it plays — the music ducks automatically. Or just pick a track and let it carry the journey.

  • Live narration recording with auto-muted music
  • Great royalty-free soundtracks built in — free for you to use wherever you share
  • Plus map theme, aspect ratio & resolution controls

Private by architecture

It can't leak what it never sends.

Roamango isn't private as a promise — it's private by design. There's no server to upload to, so your photos and locations physically stay on your iPhone.

  • No accounts, everNo email, no login, no password. Just open it and go.
  • No cloud uploadsAll processing and rendering happens on-device. The internet is only touched to fetch map tiles for a trip's area.
  • Read-only accessRoamango reads your library through Apple's standard photo permission and never modifies a single file.
  • Nothing trackedNo analytics on your photos, your places, or their contents. Your journeys are yours alone.
